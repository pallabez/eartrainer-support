EarTrainer Support

EarTrainer is a functional ear-training app for iPhone — it teaches you to hear each note's place in the key, not just distances between notes.

Get help

The fastest way to reach us is to open an issue on GitHub. Bug reports, questions and feature requests are all welcome.

Common questions

Where is my practice data?

On your device, and only there. EarTrainer has no accounts and makes no network requests — see the privacy policy.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open any locked exercise or level and tap Restore Purchase on the unlock screen. There is no account to sign into — it uses the Apple ID already signed into the App Store on your device.

Does the unlock work for my family?

Yes. The one-time unlock has Family Sharing enabled, so everyone in your App Store family group gets it with your single purchase.

No sound?

EarTrainer plays audio even with the ring/silent switch on. If you hear nothing, check the volume buttons and whether your audio is routed to a Bluetooth device (AirPods in another room are the usual culprit).

Privacy

EarTrainer collects nothing — read the full privacy policy.
