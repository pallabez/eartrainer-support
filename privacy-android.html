Pawry for Android — Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026 · applies to the Android app (com.pawry.eartrainer). The iPhone app has its own policy.

Pawry collects no data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

Pawry has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not use the Android advertising ID. The app makes no network requests of its own, so you can practice offline.

Your practice data stays on your device

Everything the app stores (practice answers, statistics, streaks, level progress and settings) lives only in the app's storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in your own device backup; that is controlled by your device settings and Google, not by Pawry, and we cannot see it.

Purchases

The one-time Pawry Full unlock is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; Pawry never sees your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase is owned, so it can unlock the content. Google's privacy policy governs the transaction.

Permissions

Notifications: only if you turn on the daily practice reminder. The reminder is scheduled on your phone; there are no push servers. Run at startup: lets the app re-schedule that reminder after your phone restarts. Pawry does not use the microphone, camera, location or contacts.

Children

Pawry collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us via the support page.
